Universite de Tunis El Manar - Library Rules.
Opening hours: Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 1pm. Closed Sunday and on public holidays. During the examination period the library stays open until 10pm on weekdays.

Borrowing. A Licence student may borrow 3 books for 14 days. A Master student may borrow 5 books for 21 days. A doctoral student or member of staff may borrow 10 books for 30 days.

A loan can be renewed twice online unless another reader has reserved the book. Late returns are fined 500 millimes per book per day, capped at 20 dinars. Borrowing is suspended while a fine is outstanding.

Reference works, theses and periodicals are consultation-only and cannot leave the reading room. A lost book is charged at its replacement cost plus a 10 dinar processing fee.

Universite de Tunis El Manar - Admissions.
Undergraduate admission is through the national orientation system after the baccalaureate. Applications open on 1 July and close on 31 July. The minimum score depends on the programme and is published each year in August.

Master admission is by file. The application window is 1 June to 15 July. A file contains: the Licence diploma, transcripts for all 6 semesters, a CV, and a motivation letter. Shortlisted candidates are interviewed in early September.

International students apply through the Ministry of Higher Education and must provide an equivalence certificate for their diploma. The equivalence takes about 8 weeks, so applying before 1 June is strongly advised.

Re-enrolment for continuing students runs from 1 September to 30 September and is done online. A student who misses re-enrolment loses their place for the year.

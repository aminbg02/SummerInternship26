BIAT Bank Accounts and Fees.
Account types: Compte Cheque (current account, 5 dinars/month), Compte Epargne (savings, no monthly fee, 3 percent annual interest), and Compte Devise (foreign-currency account for expatriates).
Opening an account requires a national ID or passport, proof of address, and a minimum deposit of 20 dinars. Account statements are free in the BIAT Net app; a paper statement by post costs 2 dinars per request.

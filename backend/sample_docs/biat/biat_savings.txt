BIAT Savings and Investment.
The standard savings account pays 5.2 percent annually, calculated daily and paid quarterly. There is no minimum balance and withdrawals are unrestricted.
A fixed-term deposit pays 7.1 percent for 12 months or 7.8 percent for 24 months, with a minimum of 5000 dinars. Breaking a fixed term early forfeits all interest accrued in the current period, though the capital is always returned in full.
A youth savings account for under-18s pays 6 percent and requires a parent or guardian as co-signatory. It converts automatically to a standard account on the holder's 18th birthday.

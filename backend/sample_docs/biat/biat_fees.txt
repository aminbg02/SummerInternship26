BIAT Schedule of Fees.
Current account maintenance: 6 dinars per quarter, waived if the average balance stays above 2000 dinars.
Domestic transfer: free in the app, 3 dinars in a branch. International transfer: 0.4 percent of the amount, minimum 25 dinars.
Card replacement: 20 dinars, or free if the card was faulty. Emergency replacement abroad is 90 dinars. A returned direct debit costs 15 dinars. An unarranged overdraft is charged at 18 percent annually plus a 25 dinar fee, applied once per month at most.
A statement older than 12 months, requested on paper, costs 5 dinars per statement.

GlobalNet Internet Plans.
Home fiber: Home 100 (100 Mbps, 35 dollars/month), Home 300 (300 Mbps, 50 dollars/month), Home Gig (1000 Mbps, 75 dollars/month). All home plans include a free dual-band Wi-Fi 6 router and unlimited data.
A 12-month term waives the 60 dollar installation fee; without a term, installation costs 60 dollars and the plan is billed month-to-month.
Students and seniors qualify for a 15 percent discount on any Home plan with proof of eligibility submitted through the customer portal.

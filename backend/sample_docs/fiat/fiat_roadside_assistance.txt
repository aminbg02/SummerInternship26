FIAT Roadside Assistance.
Roadside assistance is included free for the 2 years of the manufacturer warranty and can be renewed afterwards for 120 dinars per year.

Call 80 100 200, free, 24 hours a day and every day of the year. The target arrival time is 45 minutes in an urban area and 90 minutes elsewhere.

It covers: roadside repair, towing to the nearest authorised workshop, a flat battery, a puncture, a lost key, and running out of fuel (the call-out is covered, the fuel itself is not).

If the vehicle cannot be repaired the same day and you are more than 100 km from home, a hotel night or the onward journey is covered up to 200 dinars.

Assistance is refused for a vehicle being driven without a valid inspection certificate, or for repeated call-outs for the same unrepaired fault.

FIAT Scheduled Servicing.
Petrol engines are serviced every 15000 km or 12 months, whichever comes first. Diesel engines are serviced every 20000 km or 12 months.

An intermediate service costs 180 dinars and covers the engine oil, the oil filter, and a 20-point inspection. A full service costs 350 dinars and adds the air filter, the cabin filter, the spark plugs and a brake fluid check.

The timing belt must be replaced at 120000 km or after 5 years. This is not optional: a broken belt destroys the engine and is not covered by the warranty.

Missing a scheduled service does not automatically void the warranty, but a failure caused by the missed maintenance will not be covered. Keep the service book stamped at every visit.

A courtesy car is provided free for any service booked to last more than one day.

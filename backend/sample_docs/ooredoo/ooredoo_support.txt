Ooredoo Customer Support.
Call center is available 24/7 on 1111 (free from an Ooredoo line). Check your balance and remaining data by dialing *100# or in the My Ooredoo app.
To recharge, buy a scratch card and dial *120*<code>#, or top up in the app. If your 4G is slow, toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds to re-attach to the network; a persistent outage should be reported in the app under Network Status.

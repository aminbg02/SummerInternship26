Ooredoo Youth and Student Offers.
Customers aged 16 to 25 with a valid student card qualify for the Youth plan: 40 GB of data, unlimited calls and SMS, for 22 dinars per month.
The plan includes free data for study platforms and campus Wi-Fi roaming at partner universities. Proof of enrolment must be re-submitted every academic year, by 31 October; if it lapses, the line reverts to the standard plan at 32 dinars and the customer is notified 14 days beforehand.
A Youth line cannot be part of a family plan pool at the same time.

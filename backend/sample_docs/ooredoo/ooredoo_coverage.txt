Ooredoo Coverage and Network.
Ooredoo operates 4G across 96 percent of the population and 5G in Tunis, Ariana, Sousse and Monastir. Indoor coverage in dense urban areas is supported by small cells.
A customer reporting weak indoor signal may request a free signal booster, subject to a technical survey. The survey takes up to 10 working days. If the survey finds no fault on the network side, the booster is offered at 39 dinars instead of free.
Network status is available in the My Ooredoo app under Network, refreshed every hour.

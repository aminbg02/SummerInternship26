Ooredoo Customer Care Standards.
Call centre hours are 8am to 10pm, 7 days a week, on 1200 from an Ooredoo line and free of charge. Average answer time is targeted at under 90 seconds.
In-store appointments can be booked in the app and are held for 15 minutes past the booked time.
A fault reported on a fixed line is diagnosed remotely within 4 hours. If an engineer visit is needed, it is offered within 3 working days, in a 4-hour window chosen by the customer. A missed engineer appointment is compensated at 20 dinars.

Topnet Moving Home.
A move should be notified at least 15 working days in advance, in the app or by phone. The transfer fee is 40 dinars, waived for customers of more than 24 months.
If the new address has fibre coverage, the service is transferred with no change to the plan or the contract term. If it has only ADSL, the customer may downgrade to the equivalent ADSL plan and pay the lower price, with no penalty.
If the new address has no Topnet coverage at all, the contract is cancelled without penalty on proof of the move, such as a lease or a deed. The router must be returned within 14 days.

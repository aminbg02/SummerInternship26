Topnet Billing and Subscription Changes.
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and are due within 15 days. Pay in the Topnet app, online by card, by bank standing order, at a Topnet agency, or at a post office.
Service is throttled to 1 Mbps after 20 days overdue and suspended after 30 days. Reconnection costs 10 dinars and the line is restored within 4 hours of payment. A line suspended for more than 90 days is terminated and a new installation is required.
Upgrading a plan takes effect immediately and the difference is prorated on the next invoice. Downgrading takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. Moving home is free within a covered area and takes 5 business days.
A subscription can be paused once per year for up to 3 months, for example during a long absence, at 10 dinars/month to keep the line reserved.

Topnet Speed and Line Quality.
Advertised speeds are the maximum achievable on the line. Topnet guarantees a minimum of 80 percent of the advertised download speed on fibre, measured over a wired connection.
If a wired speed test taken 3 times on separate days shows less than 80 percent, the customer may report a fault. If Topnet cannot restore the speed within 30 days, the customer may downgrade to the matching lower plan and be refunded the difference for the affected period, or leave without penalty.
Wi-Fi speeds are not guaranteed, as they depend on the device, distance and interference.

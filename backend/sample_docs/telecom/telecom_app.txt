Telecom Mobile App.
The app allows a customer to view their invoice, check remaining data, top up a prepaid line, change plan, and report a fault.
Data usage in the app is refreshed every 15 minutes and may lag real usage slightly; the invoice is always authoritative. A plan change made in the app takes effect on the first day of the next billing cycle, except for an upgrade, which takes effect immediately with the difference prorated.
The app requires the SIM to be active. If a customer is locked out, an SMS reset code is sent to the registered number and is valid for 10 minutes.

Telecom Technical Support.
Support is available 24/7 for Fiber Pro and Fiber Max subscribers, and 8am-8pm for other plans. Phone: 800-TELECOM. 
Router reset: hold the reset button for 10 seconds until the lights blink; the router reboots in about 2 minutes. The default Wi-Fi name and password are printed on the label on the underside of the router. 
If your internet is down, first check for outages in the Telecom app under Service Status. A blinking red light means no fiber signal - contact support to schedule a technician.
